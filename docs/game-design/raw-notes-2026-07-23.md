## Terminology / storage

> Silly tavern calls player characters Personas and the v2 character card
> character npcs Characters. Since we are storing save data in these, it's
> worth noting the differences.

## Re-encounter priming

> We *will* need some sort of system to prime the characters with fresh
> scenario instructions when they are encountered again several days later,
> otherwise they will continue their last seen scenario like no time has past.
> So when visited they will need to be primed with briefly: How they know the
> user character, what their relation is, how much time has passed since last
> meeting, and what they have been up to while you were apart.

## Party / tags / NPC stats

> Stats for player: inventory, location, party list, impregnation based xp

## Natural-language stat projection

> The implications of stats should be displayed to the llm characters and
> narrator in a consistent programmatic way, but in natural language. Like so:
>
> `{{char}}'s current affection value towards {{user}} is {{affVal}}, which means {{affValDesc}}.`
> `{{char}}'s current pregnancy status is: {{isPreg}}, with {{pregNum}} {{pregStage}}. This means that {{char}} {{pregDesc}}.`
>
> Which parses to, e.g.: "Lisa's current affection towards Dyna is 110%, which
> means she trusts him completely to always have her best interests in mind,
> she cherishes her time spent with him, and truly loves him."
>
> "Lisa's current pregnancy status is: Pregnant, with 3 pregnancies at First
> Trimester. This means that Lisa has only just started to show obvious signs
> of pregnancy, with a small baby bump she looks slightly pregnant, and she is
> only just now starting lactation. She feels a flutter in her stomach as they
> continue to grow rapidly."

## Main character stats

> Ruggedness, Charm, Craftiness, Virility (+ possible fifth, undecided)
>
> Breeding mechanics like Haramase Sim.
>
> **Ruggedness** is stamina, can cum 1 time per point of it. Also for strength
> tests. Stamina is also the hp pool. Resource for casting spells, buying
> items, and leveling up.
>
> **Charm** is how likeable you are, how attractive; affects chance for girls
> to find what you say reasonable or agreeable when they contest you or are
> untrusting. A roll is always made when escalating sexual encounters.
>
> **Craftiness** is mostly your ability to use magic spells or abilities, but
> is also the intelligence stat for problem solving and plan making. It can
> help you read people's true intentions, can give you clues for when someone
> is being dishonest. And serves as your daily mana pool to spend on wondrous
> magic spells.
>
> **Virility** ties to the main goal and resource of the game, pregnancies.
> It is your reward multiplier: for every point of virility, another chance
> roll is made at the chance for fertilization. (20% chance x3 virility —
> "1.2^3 = 72.8% chance for at least one pregnancy" [see core-mechanics.md
> for corrected math])

## XP and progression

> Level up xp is awarded by sexual encounters. Easy lays are worth less. More
> dangerous difficult lays are worth more. Bard experience, lol. Banging the
> cranky dragon is worth more than the village girl.
>
> To progress in the game, you must help girls carry pregnancies to term, one
> birth gives one Power Token? All stats and rolls are handled with the

> be prompted in such a way that it knows it is in a game somewhat, so that it
> knows to prompt for rolls when a task is hard.

> Maximum text length scales with power. Bigger version of mind control spells
> can perhaps insert into the character card.

## Interaction philosophy

> I want to minimize the amount of commands needed, with them only as
> fallbacks. I want all interaction to be through GM function calls to
> facilitate player interaction with the world through parsed free-form
> roleplay. Or clean inline buttons when things are simple: "talk, move, shop,
> wait, rest, etc."
