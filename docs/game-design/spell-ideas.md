# Spell Ideas — the raw list

_Working document, pass 1 of 3. This is the **idea pile**: names and fiction
only, no mechanics, no costs. Stolen freely from D&D, Pathfinder, anime,
folklore, and this game's own obsessions. **Add yours anywhere** — mark them
however you like._

**The pipeline:**
1. **This document** — brainstorm A TON of spells, colorful natural language.
2. **The mechanics & economy pass** — walk every spell: what engine levers does
   it pull (stats, statuses, locations, information, narrative guidance)? What
   should it cost? Settle the economy questions at the bottom.
3. **The Spell Artisan** — distill pass 2 into an LLM role that turns spoken
   *vibes* into formulaic spell records: instructions, restrictions, rulesets —
   **never examples for it to reach for**. Success test: it can recreate every
   spell on this list from its description alone.

## Cantrips & Small Wonders
- **Sparklight** — a mote of dancing light follows you, bright as a lantern.
- **Tidy** — dust flees, stains lift, clothes dry and press themselves.
- **Warm Touch** — heat a drink, a bath, a bed, or the space between two people.
- **Bloom** — a living flower opens in your palm. The oldest courting trick there is.
- **Whisperwind** — your murmured words arrive at an ear one room away.
- **Copper Trick** — a coin walks your knuckles, vanishes, reappears behind her ear. Showman's magic.
- **Sugar on the Tongue** — food tastes like it was cooked by someone who loves you.
- **Ember Finger** — light a candle, a pipe, a campfire with a snap.
- **Firefly Veil** — a drifting cloud of lightmotes; ambience for an evening that matters.
- **Mirror's Favor** — for an hour, every mirror shows you at your best angle.

## Combat & Evocation
- **Ashen Bolt** — a dart of roaring cinder.
- **Skybolt** ⭐ — the caster SHRIEKS, golden light erupting from his raised hand, and a bolt of true lightning answers from the heavens — deafening, blinding, utterly unmistakable. Only works under open sky.
- **Frostbite** — cold seizes joints; the target slows, shivering.
- **Stone Fist** — your hand gauntlets itself in living rock.
- **Thunderclap** — a concussive crack staggers everything near you.
- **Razor Petals** — a whirlwind of petal-shaped blades.
- **Gravemist** — choking grey fog that claws at lungs.
- **Sunlance** — a beam of noonlight that burns a line through the dark.
- **Kindled Blade** — your weapon takes fire and keeps it.
- **Winter's Grip** — ice roots a foe's feet to the ground.
- **Backdraft** — the next blow that lands on you detonates against its giver.
- **Meteor Call** — a ritual. The sky delivers. Everyone remembers where they were.

## Wards & Protection
- **Ironhide** — skin takes the temper of forged metal.
- **Turnaside** — the first blow meant for you this day misses.
- **Hearth Ward** — a dwelling refuses entry to those who mean harm within.
- **Silent Bell** — a ward that rings only in your mind when crossed.
- **Oathshield** — harm meant for someone you have sworn to protect finds you instead.
- **Witchlock** — a door locks against everything but your hand.
- **Wardlight** — beasts and worse keep beyond the circle of your fire.
- **Anchor** — nothing teleports, summons, or spirits anyone out of this place.
- **Chastity Ward** — a sealing sigil: the wearer's release is locked away until the condition is met.

## Divination & Revelation
- **Scrying Bowl** — still water shows you a person you know, wherever they are, whatever they're doing.
- **Heartsight** — look at her and *know* how she truly feels about you. Whether you wanted to know or not.
- **Moonsight** — read the tide of her cycle at a glance: fertile, safe, or between.
- **Quickening Sight** — see at once whether a womb has caught, and what it carries.
- **Seeker's Nose** — hidden doors, false walls, buried things itch at your attention.
- **Bloodhound's Word** — speak a name over a footprint or belonging; know which way they went.
- **True North** — your body always knows the direction of one chosen person.
- **Whisper of the Walls** — a room tells you the last thing that happened loudly within it.
- **Liar's Bell** — a chime only you hear, whenever a spoken lie passes nearby.
- **Appraiser's Eye** — an item confesses what it is, what it does, what it's worth.
- **Life-Light** — every living soul nearby glows through walls for a breath.
- **Auspice** — cast the bones over a plan; receive one true omen about how it goes.
- **Dowsing** — water, ore, or the herb you need tugs at the rod in your hands.

## Messages & Bonds
- **Sending Thread** — twenty-five words to anyone you know, anywhere; their reply rides the thread back.
- **Name on the Wind** — she hears you speak her name, gently, wherever she is.
- **Lover's Knot** — tie it with her consent: each of you feels the other's strongest feelings across any distance.
- **Dream Post** — leave a message that unfolds inside her next sleep.
- **Beacon Brand** — a mark on skin (given, or *earned*); you always know where its bearer is.

## Charm & Emotion
- **Silver Tongue** — your words arrive pre-polished; charm shines brighter for a while.
- **Smolder** — your gaze carries heat; whoever holds eye contact feels it low in the belly.
- **Rose-Tinted Veil** — the air around you goes soft and warm; hearts nearby lean toward wanting.
- **Courage in a Cup** — bottled boldness for the one who drinks it.
- **Unbridled** — the greater draught: every filter, every inhibition, every polite hesitation dissolves at once. The drinker acts utterly untamed until it runs its course.
- **Cold Water** ⭐ — douse desire — yours or another's — like a snuffed candle. (The horny companion must be made to LOCK IN for the dungeon.)
- **Softened Heart** — for a time, her guard toward you sits lower than she'd ever allow sober.
- **Balm of Grudges** — an apology lands twice as deep; wounds of the heart close faster.
- **Dread Mien** — you seem, briefly, like something out of an old story about the dark.
- **Laughing Fit** — helpless, gasping, undignified laughter. A fight-ender and a party trick both.

## Mind & Compulsion
- **Whispered Suggestion** — one reasonable-sounding idea planted so gently she thinks it hers.
- **Come Hither** — a crooked finger she feels from across the town. Her feet argue with her.
- **Mesmer's Lantern** — a swaying light; the watcher stands entranced until it stops.
- **Sleep of the Meadow** — a warm drowse no one fights for long.
- **Forget-Me** — the last few minutes blur like a dream on waking.
- **Tongue-Tie** — a secret that cannot be spoken, no matter how she tries.
- **Geas** — a sworn task becomes a chain: welts of compulsion until it is done.
- **Puppet String** — for one breath, her hand moves as you will it.
- **Obedience Knot** — one command that *must* be obeyed. The darkest knot in this book.

## Illusion & Glamour
- **Masque** — wear another face until dawn.
- **Second Skin** — your clothes seem to be any clothes; rags to finery, finery to rags.
- **Glamour of the Evening** — beauty turned up like a lamp; heads follow you through a room.
- **Fetch** *(folklore's word for one's walking double)* — a pure illusion-clone of you: it walks, gestures, seems to murmur — and bursts like a soap bubble at the first touch.
- **Veil of Dull Eyes** — you become profoundly unremarkable; eyes slide off you.
- **Moonlit Stage** — dress a place in an ambience: candlelight, falling petals, distant music.
- **Phantom Chorus** — sounds that aren't: footsteps above, a voice down the hall, wolves that never were.
- **Hidden Pocket** — a carried thing cannot be found on you by searching hands.
- **Mirage Door** — a convincing exit that leads eight feet into a wall.

## Healing & Body
- **Mend** — flesh knits, bruises fade, breath comes easier.
- **Deep Mend** — the body remade whole, whatever was wrong.
- **Purge** — poison, venom, and sickness burn out of the blood at once.
- **Second Wind** — exhaustion lifts like a coat taken off.
- **Sober Up** — cruel, instant, occasionally necessary.
- **Vigor of the Ox** — strength swells beyond your frame's honest limit.
- **Nimble Fingers** — hands grow wise; locks, knots, and needles obey.
- **Painless** — the body postpones its complaints until tomorrow.
- **Lover's Mend** — healing passed mouth to mouth, skin to skin; it mends the giver a little too.
- **Restful Trance** — an hour of trance worth a night of sleep.

## Fertility & Breeding *(the house school)*
- **Kindle Womb** — warmth gathers low in her; her body readies itself beyond its season.
- **Seed of Legend** — his seed thickens with old power; what takes root, takes root *strongly*.
- **Blossom Out of Season** — her cycle turns under the spell like a tide under the moon — her fertile window opens *now*.
- **Fallow Field** — a courtesy and a mercy: nothing takes root while the ward holds.
- **Twin Star** — when conception comes, it rarely comes alone.
- **Heat of the Vixen** — a beast's season kindled in a woman's blood: days of heat, hunger, and shameless want.
- **Consecrated Union** — a blessing spoken over a bed: the next joining in it is *meant*, and the world bends to agree.
- **Cradle Ward** — no harm, hex, or misfortune touches what she carries.
- **Milkflow** — her body remembers how, whether or not it has reason yet.
- **Womb of Stone** — time stops for what grows within; it waits, safe, until the spell is lifted.
- **Soulgem Ripening** — the crystal curse coaxed toward its strange conclusion — or unwound thread by thread.
- **Self-Conception** — palm flat on her lower belly, the caster drives her body through every stage of motherhood in moments: nipples darkening, breasts swelling heavy with milk, stance widening as weight settles into the cradle of her hips and fills her womb — and then the caster is GONE, clothes collapsing in a heap, his perspective folding inward to the warm dark of her womb as the unborn weight she now carries. The GM narrates the experience from within until the spell ends the only way it can: by being born — whereupon he rapidly regrows to his full size, naked and slick with amniotic sheen. Mechanically she carries +1 pregnancy at Birth Overdue (130%). Costs one Power Token above its mana price.
- **The Borrowed Stag** *(name workshop open — "Futa Hex" as placeholder)* — she gains a cock and, with it, the knowledge of exactly how to use it, her effective virility equal to her current fertility ÷ 10 rounded up. Any child she sires on another woman while spellbound pays the CASTER the XP and eventual Power Tokens — but the pregnancy is forever labeled with its true source.

## Tantric & Intimate
- **Tantric Siphon** ⭐ — her pleasure feeds your well: each of her peaks returns a measure of mana. (Pleasure to mana — resource systems holding hands.)
- **Unsleeping Flame** — the fire doesn't bank after release; he is ready again before she's caught her breath.
- **Shared Skin** — for an hour, each feels what the other feels. Nobody stays composed.
- **The Third Hand** — a phantom touch you steer from across the room. She knows exactly whose it is.
- **Threadbare Patience** — the edge held *just* short, as long as the caster pleases. Begging optional but likely.
- **Lock and Key** — while the sigil holds, only its keeper can grant the wearer release.
- **Honeyed Skin** — she tastes of warm honey wherever lips land. Or he does.
- **Aphrodite's Tide** — the whole room breathes slower, sits closer, wants harder.
- **Velvet Hours** — a shared trance where an evening pours like syrup — an hour outside holds a night within.

## Transformation
- **Vulpine Charm** — ears, tail, and a fox's appetite for mischief, until moonset.
- **Sculptor's Palm** — one feature reshaped for a day, as the palm suggests.
- **Skin of the Selkie** — breathe water, swim like something born to it.
- **Featherfall** — the ground negotiates instead of hitting.
- **Second Voice** — speak in any voice you have heard.
- **Beastshape** — a ritual and a risk: wear an animal whole, and remember to come back.

- **Sevenleague Stride** — the road folds politely; a day's walk in an afternoon.

- **Send Her Home** — the reverse courtesy: return her gently to where her life expects her.
- **Feather Token** — send a carried item to anyone you know, arriving like a leaf.
- **Door to Anywhere** — a ritual door drawn in chalk; it opens onto any place you have stood.

## Time
- **Stolen Hour** — an afternoon passes like a held breath, pleasantly, for everyone you choose.
- **Long Candle** — the evening simply… continues. The night defers.

- **Chronomancer's Regret** — the great ritual: one step of the world, unwound. Costs what it should.

## Summoning & Binding
- **Familiar** — a small spirit companion: eyes where you aren't, opinions you didn't ask for.
- **Unseen Servant** — invisible hands that fetch, carry, pour, and never gossip.
- **Circle of Binding** — chalk, salt, and will: what stands in the circle stays in the circle.
- **True Name** — learn what a being is *actually* called; every future contest against them tilts your way.
- **Banish** — what was called from elsewhere is sent back there, mid-sentence if need be.
- **Bargain Candle** — light it at a crossroads; something arrives to negotiate. Terms vary.

## Curses & Hexes
- **Crystal Curse** *(live)* — the bloodline turns to gemstone: soulgems where children should be.
- **Withering Glare** — strength runs out of the target like water from a cracked jug.
- **Clumsy Fingers** — everything held gets dropped; everything delicate breaks.
- **Butterfly Mind** — thoughts scatter at the worst possible moments.
- **Yielding Gate** — a hex whispered against her deepest defense: her body forgets to guard its last door.
- **Evil Eye** — one success of theirs, soured into failure as you watch.

## Nature & Beast
- **Speak with Beasts** — the wolf explains itself. You may not like the explanation.

- **Rain Call** — weather invited, not commanded. Usually accepts.
- **Grove Bed** — the forest makes you a bed a queen would envy, anywhere.

## Spirit & Memory

- **Candle for the Lost** — a restless spirit shown the way to rest. Quest magic, and kindness.

## Hearth & Courting *(domestic magic — this game respects it)*
- **Perfect Supper** — whatever's in the pot becomes the best meal she's had this year.
- **Ever-Warm Bed** — the bed is always exactly the right warmth, forever. Priceless, honestly.
- **Petalfall** — flowers let go all at once, on cue, over exactly the right moment.
- **Ringlight** — a gift catches the light like it means it. The gift *lands*.
- **Chaperone's Bane** — for one evening, nobody interrupts. Doors stick, errands arise, the world minds its business.
- **Love Letter** — the page reads itself aloud in your voice, with everything you actually felt.

## Economy questions to settle (pass 2)

The pricing axes, including the ones that name themselves:

- **Base tiers** — cantrip / lesser / greater / ritual; what does each cost and what magnitude does it buy?
- **Subtlety** — does an *imperceptible* spell cost more than an obvious one? (Proposal to argue about: yes — discretion is power.)
- **Range rings** — touch → same scene → across areas → anywhere-you-know → **across dimensions**. Surcharge per ring?
- **Targets** — self / one / a chosen few / a whole room. Linear, or bulk discount?
- **The Hell's Gate question** — teleporting 3 people TO HELL = base gate + passenger fees + a *dimensional postage* multiplier? What number makes that feel appropriately enormous but reachable at high Craftiness with soulgem fuel?
- **Resistance** — contested spells (vs an unwilling target) vs consented/self/world spells: same cost, different reliability? Or cheaper *because* they can fail?
- **Duration & permanence** — a scene, a period, a day, until-condition, forever. Where does each tier cap out?
- **Reversibility** — should an undo-spell (Banish, Send Her Home, Fallow Field) be cheap on purpose, as a design mercy?
- **Fuel** — mana is Craftiness-capped and small; soulgem crystals as ritual fuel; Power Tokens for permanence?
- **Narrative-only spells** — do pure-flavor spells (Petalfall, Moment of Gold) cost 1 flat, always, to keep magic *felt* in every scene?

## Pass-2 mechanical notes (accumulating as they occur to us)

- **Whisper of the Walls** (Dyna, 2026-08-12): implementable from ENGINE TRUTH —
  walk the world's NPC schedules for everyone whose slots placed them in this
  area over the relevant window, and ALSO everyone whose slots placed them in
  *adjoined* areas (the connections graph), reported as "seen passing." The
  machinery mostly exists: `npcSlotFor(npc, day, period)` already answers
  where-was-she-when for any past slot (it's how `absencePursuits` walks an
  absence), and adjacency is `location.connections`. Layer in session events
  the schedule doesn't know (summons, escorts, lingering pins) for honesty.
- **Mind-reading spells** (Dyna, 2026-08-12) — Liar's Bell, Heartsight-family,
  and much of the Mind school need a new lever: a **mind-reading step** that
  looks through her model's THOUGHT BLOCKS — past ones already on the record,
  or her *next* reply's — with a directed instruction: "briefly report any
  deception," "parse her feelings toward me." Two wirings to explore in the
  dive: (a) **past** — a directed judge call over her recent replies +
  stored reasoning traces (reasoning-model backends keep them; on non-thinking
  backends the judge reads between the lines of her prose instead); (b)
  **future** — arm the spell, and her next reply gets a post-generation parse
  before the player sees the verdict. Tricky wiring, and a real prompt-
  engineering challenge: the Spell Artisan must be taught this as a TOOL with
  proper instructions for when and how a spell may invoke it — rules, not
  examples, same law as everywhere.

- **Skybolt** (Dyna, 2026-08-12): needs an ENVIRONMENTAL PRECONDITION lever —
  "only works under open sky" is a fact about the location. Cleanest: an
  `outdoor` location tag the world editor sets (tags already exist), with a
  judge fallback reading the scene when untagged. Its loudness is also the
  far end of the VISIBILITY axis: maximally obvious, everyone present and
  possibly adjoining areas react.
- **Forget-Me** (Dyna, 2026-08-12): leverage the machinery we already stamp —
  remove HER avatar from the `present` array of the forgotten messages (the
  Presence extension then hides them from her context specifically; other
  witnesses keep their memory), or toggle message visibility outright — plus
  a one-shot injection on her next reply: where she is, how long she's been
  here, and that she has completely forgotten what she was doing or why.
- **Disguise magics** (Masque, Second Skin — Dyna, 2026-08-12): must
  temporarily rewrite the player's NAME and persona description field. Tricky
  and sharp-edged: the entire save lives at
  `persona_descriptions[avatar].rpg_data` — mutate the description TEXT only,
  never the avatar key, and stash the original for restoration. A crash
  mid-disguise must not orphan the save.
- **THE ILLUSION PROBLEM** (Dyna, 2026-08-12 — design doctrine): *"Character
  narration is the thread of reality in chatbot roleplay — divergence in that
  corrupts the thread. Illusions are almost incompatible with the medium
  unless we can rein it back in consistently."* The cliff example: she
  experiences an illusion of falling and splatting. Requirements that fall
  out: (1) her reply-to-the-illusion must be EXCLUDED from judging — no
  damage, no reaction verdicts off false events; (2) her NEXT reply must be
  seeded with "everything is as it was before the cliff" WITHOUT naming it an
  illusion; (3) her non-dialog narration must never assert the false reality
  as world-truth. Working shape for the dive: illusions fork into a SAFE
  class (ambience/decoration — Moonlit Stage, Phantom Chorus — they decorate
  reality rather than fork it, no containment needed) and a REALITY-FORKING
  class (Fetch, Mirage Door, the cliff) which need the full containment kit:
  judge-exclusion flag on affected replies, a restoration note, and a scoped
  GM frame. Build the safe class first; treat forking illusions as the
  late-game experiment they are.
- **Self-Conception** (Dyna, 2026-08-12): levers — a pregnancy record ABOVE
  overdue (130%, +1 pregnancy on her), a POWER TOKEN surcharge on top of
  mana (new cost axis), and the big one: a PLAYER-PERSPECTIVE INTERLUDE —
  the player's agency is suspended and the GM narrates the experience from
  inside until an end-trigger (the birth) releases him: restored to full
  size, naked, slick. The interlude lever (GM-narrated player incapacity
  with a mechanical release condition) will serve other spells and combat
  states too.
- **The Borrowed Stag / Futa Hex** (Dyna, 2026-08-12): levers — a DERIVED
  STAT rule (her effective virility = ⌈her current fertility ÷ 10⌉, live,
  not copied); NPC-sires-NPC conception (the breeding graph needs an explicit
  `sire` field — today the player is implicit sire everywhere); and CREDIT
  vs SOURCE separation: XP + eventual Power Tokens pay the CASTER, but the
  pregnancy record keeps its true source forever. Name workshop open.
